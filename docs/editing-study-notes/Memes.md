## Rules stated
- A "quick meme" is the most popular kind: a short clip that reacts to the footage. It must be "kind of relevant to the gameplay", and tasteful and appropriate; "it doesn't have to be fancy" [said 01:07 to 01:48].
- Where to put one: while chopping the video you will find moments with a "punchy ending" or ones that feel they need "that extra something"; the meme goes on those [said 01:48].
- Meme audio level: the imported clip arrives in the red on the meter; press G and lower it by "about minus six decibels" (the effect: "listen to how much better that is") [said 02:09].
- Trim the meme with In/Out (I and O) before dragging it to the timeline [said 02:09].
- Put the meme where it makes sense on screen with Scale and Position in Effect Controls; flip it horizontally so a character faces the right way; crop with the handles [said 02:29 to 02:49].
- Frame hold: wherever the playhead is, the picture holds for the length of the clip [said 02:49].
- Pop-in on the footage: put an adjustment layer (same settings as the project) over all meme clips with a Transform effect; add scale and position keyframes on the frame before the meme appears, then "a couple of frames" later zoom in a little to add the next pair [said 03:10].
- DIY meme (existing movie clip or meme plus your own text labelling the characters as characters in the game): "usually" use just one per video "if that"; they get tame if overused [said 03:53].
- DIY text style: font Montserrat-style bold, centre aligned, black stroke width 14, black drop shadow (he says the fill is changed too; the frame shows white fill) [said 04:54, seen 05:18].
- DIY text tracking: put cuts in the text where the movie cuts; keyframe position, go forward about 3 frames each time and move the text, so keyframes are created automatically [said 05:14].
- Unlink the clip (Ctrl+L) to resize or silence only the audio and remove unwanted movie dialogue; keep the original slap sound effect [said 04:33].
- Crop/scale to hide a watermark [said 04:33].
- Flash meme (reaction image + text, with a screen shake and flash): the image starts on the zoom-in; text is the same preset, centred, snapped with Ctrl [said 05:59 to 06:19].
- Distortion on the text: a Turbulent Displace preset from his own pack, amount set to 20, then the pre-made keyframes made "less intense" [said 06:19 to 06:39].
- Nest image + text, put a Transform on the nest and keyframe scale so it pops in "just a little bit"; set Bezier velocity so the left keyframe is "squeezed in" and the right one stretches out [said 06:39 to 06:59].
- Screen shake: position keyframes every "three or four frames", alternating top and bottom of the frame, for "about eight frames or so" and then back to normal (last keyframe resets position) [said 07:19 to 07:40].
- White flash: a colour matte (white), "about 8 frames long", placed just over where the meme enters; opacity keyframes 0, then 100, then 0; blend mode Overlay [said 08:00 to 08:22].
- Blur the gameplay behind the meme with Gaussian Blur at "about five" [said 08:22].
- Never put copyrighted music under memes on the grounds that "it's memes"; "it's still not okay". He points to a licensed music service (sponsor) with 50 percent off the first track [said 08:43, 10:24].
- Audio otherwise "comes down to personal taste" [said 08:22].
- Not stated: no number of memes per minute (only the DIY limit above), no explicit placement offset in frames relative to the punchline, no music-under-speech level.

## Against today's playbook
- retention "Point at the joke, don't bury it": confirmed and quantified. "One meme where it fits": his DIY limit is one per video "if that" [said 03:53], while the quick and flash memes are sparser than anything else he adds; his quick meme sits on a punchy ending, about 1.1 s long and the flash one about 0.9 s [seen 03:15, 06:52].
- retention.popupsPerMinute 1.0: no per-minute number from him. If a 10 minute video had one DIY meme and a handful of 1 s quick/flash memes, 1 per minute would be an upper bound, not a typical rate. Unconfirmed.
- retention.zoomScale 1.22: the transform scale of 122 percent appears on a punch-in keyframe [seen 07:01]; confirms the number, same recipe he uses on memes (zoom a little "a couple of frames" after the pop). zoomRamp 0.16 s: his pop is "a couple of frames" (about 2 to 4 frames, 0.03 to 0.07 s at 60 fps) and his flash shake moves every 3 to 4 frames over about 8 frames (0.13 s at 60 fps) [said 03:10, 07:19]. A very snappy ramp, quicker than 0.16 s.
- "Sound sells the hit": meme audio is normalised against the gameplay by pulling an imported clip down about 6 dB from where it clips [said 02:09]. This is relative to a clip already hot; an auto-editor should do the same measure, not a fixed -6.
- Flash/shake/blur recipe could be added as a "meme hit" building block: 8 frame white overlay flash (opacity 0 to 100 to 0), 8 frame position shake, Gaussian blur 5 behind [said 07:40 to 08:22].
- "Never" rule for copyrighted music under memes [said 08:43] supports a licensing warning but not a style number.

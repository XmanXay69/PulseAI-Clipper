## Rules stated
- Pull every file onto the computer and sort into three folders before importing: A-roll (talking head), B-roll (supporting footage), project files (graphics, logos, animation, stock, music) [said 01:02-01:44].
- If audio was recorded separately, sync it by aligning the waveforms by hand, mute the camera audio, and merge picture + audio into a compound clip so they move as one [said 04:07-04:48].
- Pick the best take of the intro (he did three, the third was best) and delete everything to the left of the hook; use blade (B), select (A) and snapping so the gap closes [said 04:48-05:48].
- Then go through the whole timeline and cut mistakes and rambling "as tight as I can" before adding anything [said 05:48-06:09].
- B-roll serves two jobs: it reinforces what is being said and it hides cuts and mistakes so the video feels seamless [said 06:29, 09:15].
- Set an in/out point (I and O) on the b-roll in the source window instead of dragging the whole clip and trimming handles; the b-roll is stretched to cover exactly across the cut [said 07:11-07:52].
- B-roll shot at 60 fps on a 24 fps timeline can be slowed to 40% and still look smooth (60 / 24 = 2.5, so 40%); he plays the case shot in slow motion over the cut [said 08:13-08:54].
- Music: at its default level it drowns the voice; "typically" keep the bed around -32 dB to -36 dB and he lands on about -33 dB, which gives "a really subtle ambiance" [said 11:39-12:02]. Place music on its own track under the A-roll audio and trim its end so it does not run past the export [said 11:19].
- Title text: add a fade-reveal animation on the text's in-point so it slowly fades in as the name is spoken; add titles after b-roll and music [said 12:23-13:25].
- Layer discipline: one type of content per video layer: layer 1 all A-roll, layer 2 all B-roll, layer 3 all graphics/text, more layers as the edit grows [said 13:45-14:26].
- Colour correct last-ish: warmer, more contrast, pull highlights down, sharpen, clarity, vignette, or apply a LUT; toggle the adjustment to compare [said 14:26-15:08].
- Review the whole edit before export; watch at 2x (Space then L) to save time; editors even edit at 2x [said 15:28-15:49].
- Export: at least 1080p (4K if shot in 4K); bitrate set to the highest, not "recommended" or lower; H.264 (or ProRes 422); MP4 not MOV; frame rate equal to the A-roll's (30 fps source exports at 30, 24 at 24); audio-only export as WAV not MP3 [said 16:10-17:13].
- He runs all ten steps for every video, "over 120" for Think Media [said 17:34-17:54].

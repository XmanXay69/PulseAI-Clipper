## Rules stated
- The story comes first; it is what drives a vlog [said 04:36-05:19].
- Basic story = three acts: setup, conflict, resolution ("beginning, middle, end"). He gives it as "setup, conflict, resolution", with the middle as "oh my God, what's the outcome going to be" and the end as a happy ending [said 04:57-05:39].
- It is not always literal, but he tries to find that three-act shape in every episode; "never an obligation to share every day, but could I find a three-act narrative in every day" - a vlog is not a journal [said 05:39-05:59].
- Shot mix: aim for an equal amount of handheld, locked-off (tripod) shots, and establishing/wide shots [said 06:00-06:21].
- Why each: establishing wides say where you are so the viewer feels "here with me"; locked-off lets the viewer forget the camera and listen to the words and the place; handheld gives spontaneity and urgency [said 06:21-06:41]. Non-stop shaky handheld is disorienting and claustrophobic [said 05:59-06:21].
- Camera rule: use the best camera you have at hand (big camera, 4K, wide lens, a light when he can; phone for runs; a point-and-shoot kept in the bag as the in-between) [said 06:41-07:22].
- Hold the camera steady; very shaky or nonsensical footage "goes straight in the trash", partly because it is hard to edit [said 07:22-07:44].
- Time-lapses and drone shots only earn their place if they serve the story. Two jobs: (1) transition, to get from one scene/part of the story to the next (e.g. skateboard scene, then a city drone shot or time-lapse, then the studio); (2) context, to show the wider picture of where you are and what is happening in time (drone best for this) [said 08:05-09:06].
- Ambitious pretty cinematography with no relationship to the narrative "feels out of place and gets boring very fast" [said 09:06-09:26].
- Be original; copying his recipe exactly is failing before you begin [said 10:09-10:30].
- Topics he names but does not cover: staying positive without sounding fake, sharing a bad day, keeping a 5-day trip to one arc [said 09:26-09:47].
- Test that convinced him to start: a trip vlog shot with precise "movie-like" cinematography instead of talking to camera; he credits that with the decision to start daily vlogging (card on screen "January 2015, two months before my first daily vlog") [said 03:33-04:16, seen 04:08].

## Rules stated
- Hook = the first 5 seconds of the short, the most important part; it must introduce the topic but leave the viewer with many open questions [said 04:55].
- A good hook is fast-paced, has no pauses, has quick music and captions on screen already [said 05:16].
- Progression: meet the expectation the intro set up; show the promised thing happening (his example shows the trip to get snow rather than cutting to a cooler of ice) [said 05:38-06:20].
- Climax: the single moment the topic is fulfilled; everything is set up to land there [said 06:20].
- Do not pay off the topic early. His negative example reveals the Mr Beast "secret" about 5 seconds after introducing it, so there is nothing left to wait for and viewers scroll; the answer belongs at the end [said 03:11-03:53].
- Idea and delivery are equal: "editing" = cutting, pacing, captions; "structure" = storytelling and payoffs; combine both [said 04:14-04:35].
- Pick topics for the audience of the niche; study the big channels [said 01:48-02:50]. (Strategy, not craft.)
- Posting: day of week did not matter; morning posting got noticeably higher views for him, so post when the channel's own audience-activity graph peaks (Studio > Audience) [said 07:42-08:22]. Once per day was his sweet spot; 4 per day was too much (only about half got picked up and quality dropped); 2 per week was fine but unproductive [said 08:34-09:04].
- Imitate successful creators [said 06:46].

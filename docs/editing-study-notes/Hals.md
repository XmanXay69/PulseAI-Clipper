## Rules stated
- A commentary short has three parts: clips, voice-over, "edit/add-ons" (sound effects, transitions, ins/outs); the third is what most people skip [said 01:23-01:43, 18:33].
- Keep no clip longer than 5 seconds; his clips are "two to five seconds" and he changes the clip "every 2 to 5 seconds" because viewers will not watch one thing straight for more than about 10 seconds [said 14:25-14:46].
- Clips must match what the voice-over says at that moment (voice says drivers lose kilos from dehydration, show drivers drinking) [said 15:06].
- Clip clean-up preset on every clip: exposure -2, contrast +2, sharpen +5, to make clips look crisper [said 08:14, repeated 20:58].
- Cut the voice-over so there are no gaps or silent moments [said 08:35, 20:38]. Voice-over level set to "minus one" in the editor so it does not sound like yelling (unit not stated) [said 08:35].
- Opening: a whoosh sound at the very start and a zoom-in animation on the first clip ("zoom one" in-animation), which he says raises engagement [said 07:33-07:53, 09:16].
- Hook sound: a rising "suspense" sound under the opening; at the moment it cuts into the main video, a shutter-click sound plus a glare transition [said 11:02-11:23].
- Through the video: whoosh sound lined up with a zoom-in "Pull in" transition at cuts; his three transitions are Glitch, Pull in and Glare II; he is currently using the glitch more, always paired with a glitch sound effect [said 11:44-12:04, 18:55-19:16].
- Arrows/circles to point at things: image with background removed (chroma key), animation "flash" in, "fade out" out, a "pop" sound on the entrance, and motion tracking so it follows the object [said 12:44-14:05].
- Subtle zoom on stills: do not use the preset zoom animation (it "messes it up"); instead keyframe the Transform scale: keyframe at the start, move to where the zoom should stop and add a second keyframe [said 15:28-16:51].
- End the short immediately; do not leave a random clip hanging at the end for 5 seconds because it hurts engagement (he wants people to finish without noticing) [said 16:51-17:12].
- Captions: CapCut "auto captions" (a Pro feature). Font Montserrat, size 13, bold, white (the garbled transcript also says "metallic"); a popular animated template is "Color bounce"; he likes captions in all caps [said 17:32-18:13, 20:17].
- Watermark "@channelname" text, small (font size 8), low opacity, kept on the whole video [said 09:38-10:41].
- Single-clip commentary shorts are simpler: one trending clip + voice-over + zoom-in sound effect + captions (+ optional suspense sound) [said 19:16-20:17].
- Commentary shorts are chosen because they are easiest to monetise [said 01:23]. Remix a viral short's transcript with ChatGPT, keeping length, flow and pace, but original wording [said 05:09-06:11].

## Rules stated
- Posting clips to short-form platforms is the best way to draw attention to a stream, but expect little direct conversion; the gain is growth on another platform [said 00:00].
- TikTok, Reels and Shorts "absolutely want" portrait; Twitter tolerates 16:9; Shorts can also be square [said 00:41]. His on-screen footnote at 00:49 says any format can be posted but portrait is optimal [seen 00:49].
- Workflow tip: batch the week's clips in one day (his example Sunday) and post them across the next week [said 00:41].
- Vertical layout recipe (the one he uses most): three copies of the clip stacked as layers: (1) background scaled up to fill the frame, blurred later; (2) main gameplay scaled "140 percent" and dragged down a little; (3) the facecam, cut out with an ellipse mask and placed where wanted [said 11:36-12:16]. Build once as a template sequence, then swap the footage with "Replace Footage" so cam, background and gameplay positions persist [said 12:38].
- Captions: always add them; burned-in is best, both for people without sound and as an "additional attention grabber"; TikTok adds its own auto captions and Instagram has caption stickers but burned-in is still better [said 13:19-13:39].
- Premiere auto-captions are imperfect on a Twitch clip (game sound, music, other voices) so fix wrong words by hand (example: "smite" for "smart") [said 14:00]. When generating, pick a short frequency of "a couple words at once" via maximum length in characters [said 14:00-14:20], then move the caption to the middle of the frame and make it bigger [said 14:20-14:44].
- In free web tools, free output is capped: StreamLadder free 720p 60 fps, Plus 1080p 60 fps at $4/month billed yearly [said 03:25-04:05]; Crossclip free is 720p 30 fps with a watermark plus an outro logo clip [said 09:51, 10:54]; InShot can save 1080p 60 fps, web tools cannot [said 17:10]; CapCut adds an outro that can be removed free [said 19:53].
- Baked-in name stickers are "not totally necessary" since TikTok and Instagram let you add text on top [said 03:45].
- Phone path: copy the Twitch clip link, download via a clip downloader, then InShot (PiP of the same clip, circular mask for the cam, zoom the gameplay "a little", blur or fill background) [said 15:06-16:28]; he prefers InShot to CapCut because CapCut "snaps" [said 18:31].

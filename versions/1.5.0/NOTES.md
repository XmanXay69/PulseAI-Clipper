## PULSE 1.5.0

### A studio look
The whole app got a design pass — the same dark, dense layout, now with the polish of a paid suite:
- **Lit surfaces** — cards, tiles and popovers are raised with soft shadows and a light catch along the top edge;
  the Home hero sits on a softly lit banner.
- **Gradient controls** — primary and AI buttons are lit gradients with a glow on hover and a press; the playhead
  and the sidebar's active marker share the same pulse gradient.
- **Grouped sidebar** — Create / Library / Deliver sections, an accent bar on the active page, a gradient wordmark.
- **Breadcrumb top bar** — *Project › Page* with a save-state dot, and a recessed search field.
- **Pill page bar** — the Media → AI Clips → Edit → Captions → Deliver flow as a segmented pill, with Edit My VOD
  as a proper AI button.
- **Richer timeline** — clips are gradient-shaded with a light edge, a capsule name tag, a white glow when
  selected and a drop shadow while dragging; every track header carries its kind's colour stripe; the playhead
  glows; the play button lights up while playing and the timecode sits in a recessed well.
- **Empty states** with a ringed icon, and lifted hover states on every card and tile.

### Instant replays and the rest of 1.4.0
This build also carries everything from 1.4.0 (released together): instant replays as a new ask-first extra, slow
push-ins on static stretches, B-roll that takes a beat over from a meme, beat-synced music that never stops
early, "Approve the cut" that analyzes first, and the smaller fixes — see [1.4.0](../1.4.0/NOTES.md).

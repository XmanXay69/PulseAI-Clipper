## PULSE 1.4.0

### New extra: instant replays
- **Instant replays** (asked for like the other extras) — on gameplay videos, the biggest plays are cut in again in
  slow motion right after they land: a "▶ REPLAY" tag, a whoosh, a slow cinematic push-in, then the story resumes
  exactly where it left off. Two or three per video, never the hook's moment, at least three minutes apart.

### Edits feel more alive
- **Slow push-ins** — a long stretch with no cut and no zoom of its own (20 s+) now gets a barely-there push-in
  (1.00 → 1.04 over the piece), so the frame is never completely static. Off for "Subtle".
- **B-roll takes the moment** — when a B-roll cutaway lands on a beat that also had a meme pop-up and a sting, the
  cutaway replaces them instead of stacking on top.
- **Beat-synced music never stops early** — when starting a piece on the beat pushed it past the end of its track,
  the music now continues from the top of the track instead of leaving a gap before the next chapter; music
  drops are kept.
- **Replays and cutaways don't repeat the captions** and don't count as cuts for the hidden jump-cut framing.

### Smoother
- **Approve the cut** on a video that hasn't been analyzed yet analyzes it first (with progress) instead of waiting
  forever.
- The **Edit My VOD** time estimate includes beat detection and B-roll downloads.
- The **loudness check** says when it's still leveling the audio before measuring.
- Crosstalk trims are labelled "Crosstalk" (not "Silence") in the removed-sections list.
- A project can no longer end up with the same media listed twice after an edit that reused a downloaded clip.

## PULSE 0.1.0 — preview builds

The first downloadable builds (published as `v0.1.0-build.N`):

- AI clip finding with hook → context → payoff scoring, one-click captioned 9:16 shorts
- Professional timeline, inspector, layouts and layout morphs, compound clips, multicam with live switching
- Word-level captions with styles and per-speaker colors, text-based editing
- On-device transcription (whisper.cpp built in), neural speaker detection, AI voice isolation
- Royalty-free music and sound-effects library generated on the Mac
- Screen + webcam + mic recording, export presets and batch export queue
- Drag-to-Applications `PULSE.dmg` installer (universal: Apple Silicon and Intel)

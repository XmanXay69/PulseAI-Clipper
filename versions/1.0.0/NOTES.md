## PULSE 1.0.0

### New
- **Edit My VOD** — turns a whole stream into a 10–20 minute YouTube video: cuts the dead time, keeps the
  funniest and highest-energy moments with enough context to follow, opens with a fire hook (a teaser of the
  best moment), and adds zooms, captions, meme pop-ups, sound effects and a music bed per chapter — with
  "don't over-edit" limits (Subtle / Balanced / Energetic). Writes a YouTube chapter list for the description.
- **Edit coach** — a live performance prediction for the open edit (hook, energy, payoff, pacing, ending,
  length, polish) and suggestions as you work, most with a one-click fix: start later, cut dead air, add
  captions, zooms, music or sound effects, add a cold-open hook, trim a silent ending.
- **AI performance tiers** on every clip (🔥 Viral potential · 🚀 Strong · 👍 Solid · 🛠 Needs work).
- **TikTok Sans** (TikTok's open-source typeface) is built in and the default for captions and titles, with a
  new "TikTok" caption style; "YouTube" subtitles for long-form edits.
- **Time estimates** — before analyzing ("takes about 4 min for this 1:12:00 video on this Mac") and a live
  countdown while it runs. PULSE learns your Mac's speed from every analysis.

### Better
- Clips end after the laugh or reaction instead of cutting it off, and never stop mid-word.
- A pro-editor layout: page bar (Media → AI Clips → Edit → Captions → Deliver), neutral graphite theme, the
  sidebar folds to icons on working pages, and Home leads with *Make Shorts* or *Make a YouTube Video*.

### Notes
- Meme pop-ups are text and emoji with synthesized sound effects — no copyrighted meme images or sounds.
- The performance score is a prediction from the video's own signals, not real view data.
